常见问题FAQ

为什么不开源？ Why isn't the source code public?

本应用目前仅以安装包形式发布，源码暂不开放。应用本身完全免费、无广告、无内购。古籍原文属公有领域；应用内的白话译文、训诂注释与题库为原创整理成果，受著作权保护，数据文件经加密并嵌有用于侵权取证的标识，未经授权禁止提取、复制或二次分发。

The app is currently distributed as a binary only. The app itself is completely free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. The classical source texts are public domain; the vernacular translations, annotations and quiz bank are original works protected by copyright. Data files are encrypted and carry forensic identifiers — extraction, copying or redistribution without authorization is prohibited.

需要联网吗？ Does it need an internet connection?

不需要。全部内容离线内置，阅读、背诵、做题均可在无网络环境使用；部分社区功能联网后可用。

No. All content is built in and works offline — reading, recitation and quizzes. A few community features require a connection.

会收集我的数据吗？ Do you collect my data?

应用不收集个人数据，学习记录保存在本地设备，可自行备份导出。隐私政策见应用内说明。

No personal data is collected. Study records stay on your device and can be exported. See the in-app privacy policy.

iOS 版什么时候有？ Is there an iOS version?

目前仅提供 Android 版；网页版（PWA）可在 iPhone 浏览器中离线使用。

Android only for now; the web (PWA) version can be used offline in an iPhone browser.
